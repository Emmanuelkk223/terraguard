# Demonstration script (about 4 minutes)

Use the real dashboard on the full processed dataset. State what the data are before showing results.

1. **Framing (20 s).** "MODIS and VIIRS both detect fire, but VIIRS reports several times more detections. Adding them would
   mislead, so TerraGuard harmonizes them." Open *Sensor harmonization* and show raw ratio versus cell-day ratio and the
   agreement chart: the gap shrinks but does not close, and we say so.
2. **Select an area (20 s).** Choose a region (for example Northern) in the *Area* menu; note the 5 km grid.
3. **Open the burning activity calendar (30 s).** Point out the dry-season peak and wet-season trough, hatched months with
   incomplete coverage, and ▲/▼ markers for unusual months.
4. **Select a month (30 s).** Click a flagged month. Read the panel: value, historical median, difference, baseline size, and the
   explanation sentence.
5. **Investigate an unusual period (40 s).** Open *Unusual activity*, sort by score, open a month from the list.
6. **See where (30 s).** The map shows which cells had detections and for how many days; hover for MODIS and VIIRS counts.
7. **Compare history (30 s).** *Historical analysis*: seasonal median and range, and annual totals with partial years muted.
8. **Download (20 s).** *Data & downloads*: export the calendar CSV; show the `#` header with source, method version, area and grid.
9. **Honesty close (20 s).** *Methodology*: detections are not unique fires, no predictions, what the harmonization cannot do.

Do not quote numbers from memory: read them from the screen for the selection being shown.
